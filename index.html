Run Standalone on Your Phone

TideCast is configured as a standalone Progressive Web App (PWA). Add it to your phone's home screen to launch full-screen with its own wave icon and zero browser bars:

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

1. Tap the Share icon () at the bottom of Safari.
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
3. Tap Add in the top right corner.

Android (Chrome)

1. Tap the three-dot menu () in the top right.
2. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
3. Confirm to place TideCast on your home screen.

Tip for Android Chrome: Make sure you open this file from a web link (https://... such as Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages) rather than a local file:// folder so Chrome enables native home screen installation.
